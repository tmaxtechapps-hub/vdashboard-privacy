Privacy Policy for VDashboard

Last updated: 26 October 2026

VDashboard ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android app for keeping track of your vehicles: fuel and charging, services, expenses, documents and reminders. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

Everything you enter in VDashboard stays on your device. The app has no user accounts and no servers of our own. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no trackers, and it does not send your data to us or to anyone else. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Information Stored on Your Device

The data you enter is kept in a database inside the app's private storage on your device. Depending on what you choose to record, this can include:

  • Vehicle details, such as make, model, registration plate, VIN, technical specifications, and purchase or sale dates and prices
  • Fuel and charging logs, odometer readings, services and repairs, including workshop names and phone numbers you enter
  • Expenses, and documents such as insurance or inspection records, with their numbers and validity dates
  • Reminders, notes, and app preferences (such as language, units and theme)
  • Photos and attachments you add, for example vehicle photos, receipts and invoices

We have no access to this data and cannot see, recover or delete it for you.

Permissions the App Uses

  • Camera (optional): only when you choose to take a photo of a vehicle, a receipt or an invoice. Photos are saved in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Photos: when you pick an existing image, the app uses Android's photo picker and receives only the image you select. It cannot read or scan your photo library.
  • Notifications: to show reminders for services, documents and other dates you set. Reminders are created and scheduled on your device.
  • Run at startup and background work: so reminders are rescheduled after your device restarts and stay up to date. This work runs on your device only.
  • Vibrate: for haptic feedback.

Calling a workshop or adding an appointment to your calendar opens your phone's own dialer or calendar app with the details filled in. VDashboard does not request phone or calendar permissions and does not read your contacts or calendar.

Backups, Exports and Sharing

  • Backup files and PDF exports: you can create a backup file or a service history PDF and send it with your device's share feature. It goes only where you choose (for example cloud storage, email or a messaging app), and the receiving service's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Android backup: if Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's database and preferences in your device backup. Photos and attachments are left out of cloud backup and are copied only during a direct transfer to a new phone. This backup is handled by Android and Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we do not receive it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Third Parties

VDashboard contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, uses no cookies, and makes no network requests with your data. The list of vehicle makes and models used for suggestions is built into the app and works offline.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can edit or delete any vehicle, entry, photo or document in the app at any time. To remove all app data, uninstall VDashboard or clear its storage in your device's app settings. Because we never hold a copy, nothing remains with us. If you created backup files or Android backups, delete them where you stored them.

Children's Privacy

VDashboard is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
